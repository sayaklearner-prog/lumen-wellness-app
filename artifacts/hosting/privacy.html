Lumen OS - Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 31, 2026 | Last Updated: October 31, 2026

Welcome to Lumen OS ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of your personal and biometric data. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, process, and safeguard information when you use the Lumen OS mobile application and associated services.

Core Commitment: Lumen OS does NOT sell, rent, trade, or transfer your personal or wellness data to any third-party advertisers, data brokers, or marketing platforms. All biometric and telemetry calculations are strictly used to deliver your personal bio-intelligence dashboard.

1. Information We Collect

When you create an account and use Lumen OS, we may collect the following information:

  • Google Account Information: When you sign in via Google OAuth, we receive your verified full name, email address, and profile picture provided by Google APIs.
  • Biometric & Physical Profile: Gender, height, weight, daily step goals, caloric and nutrient targets, and clinical focus selections.
  • Activity & Motion Telemetry: Continuous accelerometer steps, active calorie burn, exercise session duration, cadence, and paired Bluetooth wearable metrics.
  • Circadian Sleep & Hydration: Sleep duration, nocturnal recovery quality scores, and logged fluid intake.
  • Nutrition Logs: Caloric intake and macronutrient breakdowns (protein, carbohydrates, fats).

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information exclusively to:

  • Authenticate your identity and provide secure account access.
  • Calculate your daily composite health score and 30-day longitudinal analytics.
  • Deliver personalized AI bio-intelligence coaching briefings and actionable wellness recommendations.
  • Synchronize real-time movement and recovery telemetry across your devices.

3. Google API Limited Use Disclosure

Lumen OS's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

4. Data Sharing and Third-Party Disclosures

We do not share your personal information with third parties, except in the following limited circumstances:

  • Service Providers: Cloud infrastructure and database services operating strictly under our instruction and subject to strict confidentiality agreements.
  • Legal Requirements: If required by applicable law, regulation, legal process, or governmental request.

5. Data Security & Storage

We implement industry-standard encryption protocols (including HTTPS/TLS in transit and secure AES storage at rest) to protect your biometric and personal telemetry against unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

6. User Rights & Data Deletion

You have full ownership of your data. You may:

  • Review and edit your personal profile at any time in the app settings.
  • Request permanent deletion of your account and all associated telemetry records by contacting us at sayak24052006@gmail.com. Upon receipt, all stored records will be permanently expunged within 30 days.
